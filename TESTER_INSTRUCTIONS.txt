TEAMworks360 - TRIAL COPY (demo data only)
==========================================

This is a practice copy. It contains made-up customers, suppliers and jobs -
no real TEAM information - and everything you enter stays on YOUR computer
only. Nobody else can see it, and nothing you do can affect the real system.

BEFORE YOU START
  1. Python 3 must be installed (https://www.python.org/downloads/ - on
     Windows, tick "Add Python to PATH" during setup).
  2. Have an authenticator app on your phone (Microsoft Authenticator or
     Google Authenticator). The app requires two-factor sign-in on every account.

TO RUN IT
  Windows:  unzip the folder, then double-click  start_colleague_test.bat
  Mac:      unzip, then right-click start_colleague_test.command -> Open
            (first time only; after that you can double-click it)
  The first run installs what it needs and sets up the demo data, so it takes
  a minute or two. Your browser then opens by itself.

SIGN IN
  Username:  tester
  Password:  TestDrive2026
  The first time, the app shows a QR code (and a typed key). Scan it with
  your authenticator app, enter the 6-digit code, and SAVE the backup codes
  it shows you. Next time you sign in you'll enter a fresh code from the app.
  The "tester" account is an Administrator, so you can try every page.

WHAT TO LOOK AT / WHAT TO TELL US
  Try your own day-to-day tasks: quotes, works orders, purchase orders, goods
  in, despatch notes, inventory, and the Reporting page. Please note down
  anything confusing, slow, missing, or that doesn't match how you work, and
  send it to Simon.

  Every screen carries an orange "TEST COPY" banner so you always know this is
  the practice version.

TO START AGAIN FROM SCRATCH
  Close the black window, delete the folder  TEAMworks360-Demo  from your user
  folder (C:\Users\<you>\TEAMworks360-Demo on Windows, or your home folder on
  Mac), then run the launcher again.

TO STOP
  Close the black window that opened alongside the browser.
